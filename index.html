How to check a company yourself, in five minutes

  • Company exists? Search the name on the MCA portal — every registered Indian company is listed there with its CIN and directors.
  • Real address? Put the address into Google Maps. A hospital or factory has photos and years of reviews. A scam has a flat number.
  • Real website? A working domain with a phone number and a physical address. "Coming soon" pages and free subdomains are a warning sign.
  • Who contacted you? A company recruiter uses a company email domain, not a personal Gmail or a Telegram-only account.
  • Ask one specific question — "who is the reporting manager and what is the shift timing?" Scammers cannot answer specifics.

Privacy — in plain words

This site has no server and no database. Saved jobs and your applied list live only in your own browser's storage, and clearing your browser deletes them.

When you submit a report or post a job, the form sends that text to our email inbox so a human can review it. We do not store it on a website, we do not sell it, and we never publish your phone number.

We do not ask for Aadhaar numbers, OTPs, bank details or document photos. If anyone asks you for those in the name of a job, it is a scam — report it above.

To have a submission deleted, email us with the same text you sent and we will remove it from the inbox.

Terms, briefly

JobVibe lists jobs from employers, government portals and public job boards. We check what we can, but we cannot guarantee any employer. Always verify before you travel or pay anything.

Reviews are the personal opinions of the people who wrote them. We remove reviews that name individual managers, contain abuse, or cannot be corroborated.
