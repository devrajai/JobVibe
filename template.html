Company career portals — apply directly, no middleman

These open the employer's own careers page, which is where freshers actually get hired — not a third-party form. If a link has moved, search the company name plus “careers”.

Internships — paid and free

For students in their last year, or anyone who finished study and needs experience on paper. Listings marked “Unpaid but certified” give a certificate and letter of recommendation instead of money — only take those if you actually need the certificate.

Rule of thumb: a genuine internship never asks you to pay for “training”, “registration” or a “laptop deposit”. If money flows from you to them, walk away.

Government recruitment — official sources only

Every link below goes to the official government portal. We do not copy application forms, and we never take money for a government job. Always confirm the notification PDF on the official site before applying.

Fake recruitment warning: websites with names like “sarkari-result-online” or “govtjob-apply” often charge ₹200–₹500 for forms. Real recruitment happens only on the domains listed here, ending in .gov.in or .nic.in.

Local & daily work — pay today, not next month

For anyone who needs money this week: tailoring, plumbing, electrical repair, carpentry, painting, delivery, shop assistance, watch repair, housekeeping. No degree required for most of these, and no upper age limit.

Worker directory — hire a skilled person near you

The other side of the same idea: a worker builds a free profile with photos of their work, their rate, and whether they offer home visit or pickup-and-drop. Customers find them directly. No commission to us, ever.

Free courses that actually carry weight

Real platforms, real certificates. Where a certificate needs a paid exam, we say so plainly — no “free” claims that turn into a payment page.

A certificate alone rarely gets you hired. What gets you hired is the portfolio or the practical test — the certificate is just what makes a recruiter stop scrolling. Do the course, then build one thing with it.

Honest workplace reviews & safety

Written by people who actually worked there — about salary delays, office politics, whether the manager respects women, and whether complaints are heard. Names of reviewers are hidden. Employer names are shown only where multiple people reported the same thing.

Our safety filter: the “Safe-workplace badge” is given to employers with no unresolved harassment or wage-theft reports and a verified address. It is not a guarantee — it is a starting point. Always tell a family member where your interview is, and never attend an interview alone at an unknown address.
